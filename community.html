Living in Sioux Falls, SD

Sioux Falls is more than where I work, it’s home. Here’s what makes it a great place to live.

Life in Sioux Falls

A local’s view

Knowing a community is as important as knowing the market. Here’s where I’d start.

Falls Park

The city’s signature landmark: waterfalls on the Big Sioux River, an observation tower, and a walkable downtown just beyond.

Downtown

Shops, restaurants, breweries, and the arts, including the Washington Pavilion, all within a few walkable blocks.

Parks & Trails

Miles of paved trails and plenty of green space for biking, running, and weekend walks.

Family Fun

From the Great Plains Zoo to the Butterfly House & Aquarium, there’s always something to do with the kids.

Schools & Growth

A growing city with strong employers, healthcare, and education, and new neighborhoods developing all the time.

Surrounding Towns

Brandon, Harrisburg, Tea, Lennox, and more offer small-town feel with an easy commute to the city.
