The Sioux Falls Bucket List: 15 Must-Do Experiences

By Landon Farley · November 4, 2026 · 2 min read

I’ve spent my whole life in Sioux Falls, and the question I love most is, “What is there to do?” Here’s my bucket list for new neighbors and longtime residents alike. How many can you check off?

Outdoors

  1. See Falls Park. The lights, the water, the views, and it never gets old.
  2. Hike Palisades State Park. Quartzite cliffs, scenic trails, and a peaceful escape just a short drive away.
  3. Bike or walk the River Greenway. More than 30 miles of trails connect the city.
  4. Explore Good Earth State Park. Trails, scenery, and year-round adventure.
  5. Visit a lake. Fishing, kayaking, and sunsets are great in any season.

Culture and downtown

  1. Walk the SculptureWalk. Art around every corner of downtown.
  2. Enjoy downtown after dark. Restaurants, live music, and city lights.
  3. Attend Levitt at the Falls. Free live music with an unbeatable view.
  4. Attend a local concert or event. There’s almost always something happening.
  5. Enjoy a seasonal festival or parade. Sioux Falls loves to celebrate.

Food, family, and local favorites

  1. Visit the Great Plains Zoo. Always a hit with the kids.
  2. Explore Look’s Marketplace. Local food, shops, and a historic vibe.
  3. Grab coffee at a local shop. Support local and find your new favorite spot.
  4. Eat at a local favorite. Try classics like Phillips Ave Diner or Morrie’s.
  5. Try a new local restaurant. The food scene is worth exploring.

A few of my personal favorites

  • Favorite Saturday: Fishing or walking the trails near Tuthill.
  • Favorite neighborhoods: The Ralph Rogers area and the Historic District by McKennan Park.
  • Favorite annual event: The Parade of Homes.
  • Hidden gem: Palisades State Park.

Take it with you

My free Welcome Home to Sioux Falls packet includes a printable bucket list, along with neighborhood, school, healthcare, and weekend-trip guides. If you’re thinking of making Sioux Falls home, explore the community or let’s talk.
