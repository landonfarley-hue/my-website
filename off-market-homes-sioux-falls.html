Off-Market Homes in Sioux Falls: What They Are and How to Find Them

By Landon Farley · November 4, 2026 · 2 min read

You’ve probably heard the term “off-market” and wondered what it means. In short, an off-market home is one that’s being sold without being widely advertised on public home-search sites. Buying and selling off market is one of my specialties, so here’s the plain-English version.

Why would a seller sell off market?

  • Privacy: Some sellers don’t want strangers touring the home or the sale announced publicly.
  • Convenience: No open houses and fewer showings can mean less disruption.
  • Timing: A seller who needs a quiet, flexible timeline may prefer a smaller, more targeted audience.
  • Testing the waters: Some owners want to see if the right buyer exists before committing to a full public listing.

Why should a buyer care?

Fewer people know about these homes, so there’s typically less competition. If you’re looking in a tight price range or a popular area, hearing about a home early can make a real difference.

What are the tradeoffs?

With a smaller audience, a home may not get the broadest exposure, which matters to sellers who want maximum demand. Buyers also need an agent who actually has access to these opportunities. That’s why it’s important to work with someone experienced in this area, and to have your financing lined up so you can move quickly when something comes up.

How to get access

  1. Get pre-approved so you can act fast. Here’s how pre-approval works.
  2. Tell your agent exactly what you want: price range, areas, must-haves, and timeline.
  3. Work with an agent who is connected. I’m part of a team, which means more eyes and ears on the market and someone always available.

Thinking about selling quietly?

Selling off market isn’t right for every home or every seller. It depends on your goals, your timeline, and your local market. Reach out and we can talk through whether it makes sense for you, or whether a traditional listing will get you a stronger result.
