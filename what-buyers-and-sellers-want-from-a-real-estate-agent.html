What Buyers and Sellers Really Want From an Agent: 8 Questions to Ask Before You Hire

By Landon Farley · November 4, 2026 · 2 min read

Hiring a real estate agent is one of the bigger decisions in a home purchase or sale. Here’s what national research says people value most, followed by eight questions that will help you pick the right agent, whether that’s me or someone else.

The numbers

  • 88% of buyers worked with an agent, and 91% of sellers did, a record. (NAR 2025 Profile via Virginia REALTORS®; Inman)
  • More than 9 in 10 buyers said they would use their agent again.
  • 54% of buyers said their agent pointed out problems they might have missed.
  • 76% of first-time buyers valued the guidance they received.

What buyers value most

In an earlier NAR survey, buyers said the most valuable things an agent did were: (NAR Magazine)

  • Explain the homebuying process (61%)
  • Point out features or flaws they might miss (58%)
  • Negotiate stronger contract terms (46%)
  • Recommend good service providers, like inspectors (46%)
  • Build their knowledge of the search area (45%)
  • Negotiate a better price (33%)

HousingWire’s roundup of agents adds protecting buyers from financial risk, such as losing a deposit or missing a critical contract term, and handling the paperwork and deadlines. (HousingWire)

What sellers value most

Sellers mainly want an agent who can price the home competitively, market it well, sell it within their timeframe, and suggest improvements that raise the sale price. (NAR Magazine) Reputation is the top reason sellers choose an agent (35%), and 66% found their agent through a referral or by hiring one they had used before. (Virginia REALTORS®)

How people choose an agent

Buyers ranked experience (21%), honesty and trustworthiness (19%), and reputation (15%) as the top criteria. They also said they preferred frequent communication: personal calls (73%), property information by text or email (71%), and listing or price-change alerts (70%). (NAR Magazine) Note that some of these figures come from NAR’s 2024 survey, and the 2025 survey has newer numbers for other questions.

8 questions to ask any agent before you hire

  1. How will we communicate, and how often? Ask about calls, texts and alerts, and what to expect when you have a question.
  2. Who else is on your team, and who helps when you’re unavailable? (I’m part of a team, so someone is always available.)
  3. How do you find homes before everyone else does? Ask about off-market and pre-market options.
  4. How will you price my home, and what data will you show me? (Sellers)
  5. How will you help me avoid costly surprises? Ask how they handle inspections, repairs and contract deadlines. (Buyers)
  6. Which lenders, inspectors and other professionals do you recommend, and why?
  7. How are you paid, and what will our agreement say? Under today’s rules, buyers sign a written agreement with their agent, so ask to see it and have it explained before you sign.
  8. Can I talk to recent clients? Reputation and referrals matter most to people, so ask.
