Around town

Sioux Falls local news

What’s going on in the city that could affect where you live, drive, and invest.

  • Nov 2026

    Sidewalk repair closes lanes on Minnesota Ave and East 6th Street

    Crews are repairing sidewalks on South Minnesota Avenue between 19th and 20th Streets (starting Nov. 4, about two days) and on East 6th Street between Shepard and Cleveland Avenues (expected through Nov. 6, weather permitting). Expect lane and sidewalk closures.

    Source: City of Sioux Falls
  • Nov 2026

    “Sioux Falls Listens” community sessions continue

    Mayor Christine Erickson’s transition team is hosting public sessions where residents can share ideas: Nov. 5 (business community) and Nov. 12 (economic development) at the Dakota State University Community Room, plus a town hall on Nov. 18 at Cherapa Place.

    Source: Dakota News Now
  • Oct 2026

    City council committee studies data center rules

    A council committee is reviewing local, state, and federal rules for data centers and may propose ordinances. Public meetings are planned this month, proposed ordinances are due Nov. 27, and recommendations are expected to go to the full council on Jan. 12, 2027.

    Source: Dakota News Now
  • Jul 2026

    Christine Erickson sworn in as Sioux Falls mayor

    Erickson became the city’s 33rd mayor and the first woman elected to the office on July 17, 2026. Her stated priorities include expanding opportunities for children, affordable living, attracting jobs, and keeping the city safe.

    Source: City of Sioux Falls
