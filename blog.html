Around town

Sioux Falls local news

What’s going on in the city that could affect where you live, drive, and invest.

  • Nov 2026

    “Give Me Five” down payment program opens through SD Housing lenders

    South Dakota Housing’s Give Me Five program launched Nov. 2. Eligible first-time buyers can get assistance equal to 5% of their first mortgage as a second mortgage with no monthly payments, forgiven after five years of living in the home. Homes must cost $410,000 or less, and it’s available for a limited time through participating lenders. Read my full breakdown.

    Source: SD Housing
  • Nov 2026

    SD Housing posts new rates, with Give Me Five at 6.375% to 6.750%

    Rates effective Nov. 2 at 9 a.m. CT for a 30-year fixed mortgage list Give Me Five at 6.375% for government loans (FHA, VA, USDA RD) and 6.750% for conventional loans for first-time buyers. SD Housing says rates are subject to change, and its page does not show an APR.

    Source: SD Housing
  • Apr 2026

    Keller Williams ranked No. 1 U.S. real estate brand by sides and volume

    RealTrends Verified’s 2026 ranking put Keller Williams first in the U.S. by transaction sides and sales volume. KW-affiliated brokerages made up 38.2% of the top 500 by sides, according to KW’s announcement. Read my breakdown of what the biggest brokerages do for clients.

    Source: Business Wire (KW press release)
  • Feb 2026

    Compass and Redfin sign three-year partnership to show pre-market listings

    Compass “Coming Soon” listings now appear on Redfin with priority placement. Compass also says its clients can get a Rocket Mortgage rate reduction or lender credit.

    Source: Houston Agent Magazine
  • May 2026

    Redfin launches “Early Access” for homes before they hit the broader market

    The new search category includes Redfin-only homes and pre-market listings from Compass brands, with instant alerts for buyers and a way for sellers to test pricing and demand.

    Source: Redfin
  • Feb 2026

    Keller Williams opens its Command platform to more agent integrations

    KW announced that its Command platform would open to more integrations to give agents more choice in the tools they use.

    Source: Business Wire (KW press release)
  • Nov 2025

    NAR: 88% of buyers and a record 91% of sellers used an agent

    NAR’s 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers found that more than 9 in 10 buyers would use their agent again, and first-time buyers were just 21% of purchases, a survey low.

    Source: Virginia REALTORS® (NAR data)
  • Nov 2026

    Sidewalk repair closes lanes on Minnesota Ave and East 6th Street

    Crews are repairing sidewalks on South Minnesota Avenue between 19th and 20th Streets (starting Nov. 4, about two days) and on East 6th Street between Shepard and Cleveland Avenues (expected through Nov. 6, weather permitting). Expect lane and sidewalk closures.

    Source: City of Sioux Falls
  • Nov 2026

    “Sioux Falls Listens” community sessions continue

    Mayor Christine Erickson’s transition team is hosting public sessions where residents can share ideas: Nov. 5 (business community) and Nov. 12 (economic development) at the Dakota State University Community Room, plus a town hall on Nov. 18 at Cherapa Place.

    Source: Dakota News Now
  • Oct 2026

    City council committee studies data center rules

    A council committee is reviewing local, state, and federal rules for data centers and may propose ordinances. Public meetings are planned this month, proposed ordinances are due Nov. 27, and recommendations are expected to go to the full council on Jan. 12, 2027.

    Source: Dakota News Now
  • Jul 2026

    Christine Erickson sworn in as Sioux Falls mayor

    Erickson became the city’s 33rd mayor and the first woman elected to the office on July 17, 2026. Her stated priorities include expanding opportunities for children, affordable living, attracting jobs, and keeping the city safe.

    Source: City of Sioux Falls
