Selling Your Home in a Sioux Falls Winter: 8 Tips

By Landon Farley · November 4, 2026 · 2 min read

Many people assume they should wait until spring to list. But homes sell year-round in Sioux Falls, and buyers who are out looking in winter are often relocating, on a deadline, or simply serious about moving. Here’s how to put your home’s best foot forward when it’s cold outside.

1. Keep the walkways clear

Shovel and salt the driveway, steps, and path to the front door before every showing. A safe, welcoming entrance is the first impression.

2. Make it warm, inside and out

Set the thermostat to a comfortable temperature so buyers don’t rush through. Turn on every light, since winter days are short, and open the blinds for natural light.

3. Get great photos while you can

Professional photos are your first showing. If your yard looks its best at another time of year, ask about using seasonal photos from earlier, and make sure the interior photos are bright and clutter-free.

4. Declutter the “winter pile-up”

Boots, coats, and salt-crusted mats collect fast. Give buyers a clean entry with a tidy place for their own shoes and coats.

5. Show off the cozy features

A working fireplace, a well-maintained furnace, good insulation, and new windows are all real selling points when it’s cold. Keep the records handy for buyers who ask.

6. Deal with maintenance first

Buyers notice drafts, cracked caulking, and heating issues more in winter. Have your furnace serviced and fix small items before listing. A tidy house inspects better, too.

7. Price it to match today’s market

Seasonal timing matters, but price and condition matter more. Looking at recent nearby sales will tell us where your home fits. Request a free home value and I’ll put together a realistic number.

8. Be flexible with showings

Winter buyers often have tight schedules. The more flexible your showing times, the more chances you have to meet the right buyer. That’s where being part of a team helps: someone’s always available to coordinate.
